# The example

The ISO 9001 method with a process already at work: taking part in tenders, from the call to the requirements review
(ISO 9001, 8.2.3), with its four agents, a filled-in tender (Pentagroup and the Nordica Crediti call, made up) and
`quality/processes.md` naming it. Complaints stay «to design»: they are the first exercise with the designer.
