# The processes of the quality system

## TL;DR

Every process of the quality system, with who answers for it, the evidence it leaves and its workflow. It is the map the
internal audit checks (ISO 9001, 4.4). In the example the tender already has its workflow; complaints are to design.

- One row per process; the columns are explained in the `iso9001-processes` skill.
- The tender is the model: a process that changes hands, with the evidence in the round log.
- Mark «to design» in the Workflow column and launch the designer: it will propose how to make it a workflow.

## The processes

| Process | Purpose and boundaries | Input / output | Owner | Evidence | Workflow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Taking part in tenders | from the call found to the decision to bid, with the requirements review (8.2.3) | the call and the tender document / the sheets, the review, the decision | Account manager | `tenders/sheets/`, the review, the decision in the round log | `.mde-template/iso9001/workflows/tender.md` |
| Complaint handling | from the complaint received to the answer to the customer | the complaint / the answer, the corrective action if any | Quality manager | the complaint register, the answers | to design |
