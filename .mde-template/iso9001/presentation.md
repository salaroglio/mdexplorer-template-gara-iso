# ISO 9001

The quality system as **a set of well-made workflows**:
who prepares, who approves, and the evidence that stays.

---

## <img class="mde-icona" src="/mde-space/icone/telescopio.svg" alt=""> What it is for

- An auditor asks: who answers for this process? Who approved? Where is the evidence?
- In MdExplorer a process that changes hands is a **workflow**: the roles are written down, every round stays in git
- The method helps you describe the processes, turn them into workflows and get ready for the audit

---

## <img class="mde-icona" src="/mde-space/icone/razzo.svg" alt=""> The path

1. **The processes** in `quality/processes.md`, one per row, each with its owner
2. **A process becomes a workflow**: the designer proposes, the quality manager decides
3. **The workflow works**: every round leaves its evidence
4. **The internal audit**: the report with the evidence, management decides the actions

---

## <img class="mde-icona" src="/mde-space/icone/astronauta.svg" alt=""> Who does what

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| Quality manager | approves processes and workflow proposals |
| Internal auditor | approves the audit report |
| Management | decides the actions after the audit |

Two agents prepare (designer, internal auditor's assistant), **the people approve and decide**.

---

## <img class="mde-icona" src="/mde-space/icone/satellite.svg" alt=""> The example and the theory

- **With the example**: the tender workflow, from the call for tenders to the requirements review (8.2.3), with a filled-in tender
- **With the theory**: the clauses that matter, from process to workflow, getting ready for the audit, working with the MarkAgent
