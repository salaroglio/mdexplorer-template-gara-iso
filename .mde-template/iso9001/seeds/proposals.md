# The workflow proposals

## TL;DR

Here the designer writes the workflow proposals of the processes «to design»: the steps, who prepares and who approves,
the evidence, the clauses. A proposal that is built becomes a workflow, and `quality/processes.md` writes its path.

- One proposal per process: `proposal-<process>.md`.
- The decisions are in the round log.
- Proposals are not deleted: they say why the workflow is made that way.
