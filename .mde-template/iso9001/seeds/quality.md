# The quality system

## TL;DR

Here lives the group's quality management system: the processes, the workflow proposals, the internal audits. Every
process that changes hands becomes an MdExplorer workflow, and its rounds are the evidence for the audit.

- `processes.md`: the processes, one per row, with their workflow.
- `proposals/`: the designer's workflow proposals, before they become workflows.
- `audit/`: the internal audit plan, the reports, the actions decided.
