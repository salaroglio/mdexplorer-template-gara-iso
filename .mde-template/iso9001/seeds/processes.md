# The processes of the quality system

## TL;DR

Every process of the quality system, with who answers for it, the evidence it leaves and its workflow. It is the map the
internal audit checks (ISO 9001, 4.4).

- One row per process; the columns are explained in the `iso9001-processes` skill.
- Mark «to design» in the Workflow column and launch the designer: it will propose how to make it a workflow.
- For now there is only an example to replace with the group's real processes.

## The processes

| Process | Purpose and boundaries | Input / output | Owner | Evidence | Workflow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Complaint handling (example) | from the complaint received to the answer to the customer | the complaint / the answer, the corrective action if any | Quality manager | the complaint register, the answers | to design |
