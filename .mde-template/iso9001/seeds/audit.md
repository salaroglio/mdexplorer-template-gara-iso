# The internal audits

## TL;DR

The internal audit plan (ISO 9001, 9.2), and its reports and actions. The internal auditor's assistant prepares the first
audit «to do»; the internal auditor approves the report, management decides the actions.

- The group decides how often and on which processes: write it below.
- Whoever approves a report does not answer for the processes audited.
- Reports `audit-<date>.md` and actions `actions-<date>.md` stay here.
