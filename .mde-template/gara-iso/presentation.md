# Tender with ISO 9001

From the call for tenders to the decision to bid,
with the review of the requirements **before committing**.

*This method is in Italian: its documents, agents and workflow speak Italian.*

---

## What it is for

- A tender is often decided in a hurry, and the requirements show up after signing
- ISO 9001 asks to review them **before** committing to the customer (8.2.3)
- This method makes the review a step of the work, not a form filled in at the end

---

## The round

1. **Find the calls** — the account manager's agent finds them and sums them up
2. **Three sheets** — technical, contractual, delivery: each one from its manager
3. **Requirements review** — requirements, risks, questions for the customer
4. **The decision** — the account manager chooses: we bid or we do not

---

## Who does what

- **Account manager** — finds the calls, starts the round, decides
- **Technical manager** — approves the technical sheet
- **Legal manager** — approves the contractual sheet
- **Delivery manager** — approves the delivery sheet

The agents prepare, **the people approve and decide**.

---

## What stays on record

- The review is a document in git: who, when, on which version
- The decision is in the round register, with the approved commit
- It is the evidence the standard asks for (8.2.3.2), with no extra work

---
